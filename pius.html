Frequently Asked Questions

What happens when a class is full?
When a cabin class is fully booked, the system displays the next available date and time on the same route. Click "Check Next Flight" to see alternatives.
How do I change my booking?
Go to Book & Manage → Change Booking, enter your booking reference and last name, then update your date or class. Changes are subject to fare availability.
How long does a booking inquiry take?
Booking inquiries are typically processed within 24–48 hours. You will receive a confirmation email once reviewed.
Can I add multiple passengers to one booking?
Yes — up to 9 passengers per booking. For groups of 10+, use the Group Booking option in the footer.
How do I print my ticket?
Go to Book & Manage → Reports → Ticket Report. Enter your booking reference to generate a printable e-ticket.
