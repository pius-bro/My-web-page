Frequently Asked Questions

What happens when a class is full?
When a cabin class is fully booked, the system shows the next available date and time on the same route.
How do I change my booking?
Go to Book & Manage → Change Booking, enter your booking reference, then update your date or class.
How long does a booking inquiry take?
Booking inquiries are typically processed within 24–48 hours. You will receive a confirmation email once reviewed.
Can I add multiple passengers?
Yes — up to 9 passengers per booking. For groups of 10+, use the Group Booking option in the footer.
